I am an Assistant Professor at Osaka University working on software engineering and automated program repair.

My research focuses on improving the reliability of software systems using:

- Large Language Models for Software Engineering
- Automated Program Repair
- Fuzzing and vulnerability repair
- Mining software repositories
- Empirical Software Engineering

I am particularly interested in understanding how LLMs can assist developers in debugging, repairing, and explaining software defects.
